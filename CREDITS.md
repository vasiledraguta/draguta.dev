# Credits

## Background footage

The background video (`public/background.av1.webm`, `public/background.webm`) is
edited from short clips of **Vinland Saga**, seasons 1 and 2.

- Original manga by Makoto Yukimura, published by Kodansha
- Season 1 animated by Wit Studio
- Season 2 animated by MAPPA

The clips are used here without permission, for a personal, non-commercial site.
All rights to the footage remain with the original rights holders. If you are a
rights holder and want it taken down, open an issue or email hello@draguta.dev
and it will be removed.

The favicon set and `public/og-image.png` are cropped from the same footage and
carry the same status.
